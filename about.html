About Rebecca

Illustration rooted in emotion, softness, and story

Rebecca Olivia Speed is an illustrator and character artist creating soft, expressive worlds filled with emotion, strange little moments, and storytelling.

Her work blends sketchbook energy, dreamy atmosphere, and personality-led character design — often inspired by feelings, music, visual memories, and the tiny details that make something feel human.

From polished illustration work to unfinished notebook experiments, every piece is part of the same creative universe: emotional, imperfect, personal, and alive.

currently obsessed with: strange characters, visual storytelling, sketchbook chaos & soft colours ♡

Rebecca is available for selected commissions, collaborations, and creative projects.
